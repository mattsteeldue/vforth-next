    IDE_RTC  -- x time date

Reads the real time clock through the NextZXOS call
IDE_RTC ($01CC). date and time are in MS-DOS format,
as file dates: .FAT-DATE and .FAT-TIME print them.
x is the extra value of the call (seconds and 100ths,
on clock modules that give them); DROP it otherwise.
Without a clock module the values mean nothing: use
M_GETDATE, whose flag tells.

Available after NEEDS IDE_RTC
