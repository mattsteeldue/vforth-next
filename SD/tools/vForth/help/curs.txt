    CURS    a -- a 0

Blinks the cursor character at address a (and its
inverse/bright variants driven by the ROM FLAGS/
FLAGS2 bytes) once per video frame, via 1FRAME,
until a key is available; then restores the screen
position. Used inside ACCEPT's input loop; leaves a
0 on the stack that the caller DROPs before calling
KEY. See root CLAUDE.md "Boot Sequence" for the
1FRAME/HALT keyboard-timing background.
