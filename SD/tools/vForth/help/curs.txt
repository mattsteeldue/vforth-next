    CURS    --

Shows a flashing cursor and waits until a key is
available, then erases the cursor. The key is not
taken: KEY reads it afterwards. CURS KEY joins the
two waits into one: the cursor flashes until a key
is pressed, and KEY takes it at once. KEY alone
waits too, but shows no cursor.

CURS selects the screen (2 SELECT) and needs the
ROM interrupt, which updates the keyboard flag.
Used inside ACCEPT. See also KEY.
