    :NONAME   -- xt

Compiles an anonymous (headerless) colon definition and
leaves its execution token on the stack, e.g.

    DEFER PRINT
    :NONAME  . ;   IS PRINT

Compile the body as usual, close it with the ordinary ; .
Typical use: build a word to install with IS/DEFER!, or an
xt to store in a table, without cluttering the dictionary
with a throwaway name.

Available after NEEDS :NONAME
