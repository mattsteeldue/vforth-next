    ASK-Y/N   --

Interactive boot-time prompt: "Do you wish to load
utilities ? (Y/n)". Any key other than N/n continues,
falling through to the NEEDS lines that load REMOUNT,
WHERE, .S, EDIT, DUMP, HEAP, S", SEE, etc. N/n prints "ok"
and QUITs straight to the prompt, skipping them.

Part of Screen 11 / lib/autoexec.f, run once by AUTOEXEC
during COLD boot. Not a reusable NEEDS module: it is
defined, called, and FORGET-ten again by its own MARKER in
the same script -- look here to understand the boot
sequence, not to call it from your own code.

See root CLAUDE.md, "Boot Sequence".
