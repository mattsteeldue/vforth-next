    PLAY-ON  a1 n1 ... ak nk k --

Like PLAY, but the music plays in the background,
one step per frame interrupt, while the program
goes on. The time is counted in frames (50 or 60
a second), so it does not depend on the CPU speed.
The strings are copied (1024 bytes in all), so a
string typed with S" may be used.

    S" O4ceg))" S" O3c))" 2 PLAY-ON

A word already in ISR-XT (e.g. AFXFRAME) keeps
running after the music step. Install it first.
Errors in the first notes are reported at once;
later ones halt the music: see PLAY-ERROR.
BREAK does not stop it: PLAY-OFF does.
Disk access disables the interrupt: meanwhile the
music slows down, and it does not catch up later.

See PLAY, PLAY-OFF, PLAYING?, PLAY-ERROR.
Available after NEEDS PLAY-ON
