    SPRITE  -- a

The shared sprite structure: fill its fields, then send
them to a hardware slot with SPRITE-UPDATE.
  _xcoord   X, 9 bits: 0 to 511, 32 = left of paper
  _ycoord   Y, 0 to 255
  _spriteid the PATTERN shown, 0 to 63 (not the slot)
  _rotmir   rotate/mirror bits: SPRITE-ROT SPRITE-VFLIP
            SPRITE-HFLIP
  _pattern  palette offset in bits 7-4 (keep 0 unless
            wanted: garbage here shifts every colour)
The same structure serves every slot: the slot is the
argument of SPRITE-UPDATE.

Example:  100 SPRITE _xcoord !  2 SPRITE _spriteid !
          SPRITE 0 SPRITE-UPDATE
Available after NEEDS SPRITE.  Tutorial 053.
