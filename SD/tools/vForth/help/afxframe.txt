    AFXFRAME  --

Plays one frame of the AYFX sound effects on the three AY
chips, advancing every active channel of AFX-CH-DESC.
Machine-code routine meant to be called 50 times per
second, usually from the interrupt routine (see HELP
ISR-XT). Initialise the chips first with AYSETUP.

See HELP AFX-CH-DESC and tutorials 050 and 055.

Available after NEEDS AFXFRAME
