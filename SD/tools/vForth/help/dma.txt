    DMA  --

Module marker of the zxnDMA library (port $6B). Does
nothing: it is defined last, so NEEDS DMA succeeds only
after a complete load. NO-DMA removes the whole module.

Transfers:  DMA-COPY DMA-FILL DMA-OUT DMA-IN
Commands:   DMA-DISABLE DMA-ENABLE DMA-LOAD DMA-CONTINUE
            DMA-RESET DMA-RESET-STATUS
Status:     DMA-STATUS DMA-DONE?
Low level:  DMA! DMA@ DMA-W

Available after NEEDS DMA
