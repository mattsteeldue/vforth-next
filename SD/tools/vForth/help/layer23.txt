    LAYER23  --

Activates Layer 2 high-res: 640x256 pixels, 16
colours, 4 bits per pixel, two pixels per byte
(80K RAM, vertical bands, as LAYER22).
Re-vectors PLOT/POINT/DRAW-LINE/CIRCLE.
x = vertical 0..255, y = horizontal 0..639.
Colour is a 4-bit index 0..15 into the Layer 2
palette.

Part of the LAYER23-GRAPHICS library.
Available after NEEDS LAYER23-GRAPHICS
