    MDR  --

Show the syntax of the words that access ZX Microdrive
cartridge images (.MDR files), loaded by NEEDS MDR.
NO-MDR unloads the module.

Read: MDR-CAT, MDR-SECTORS, MDR-INFO, MDR-GET, MDR-DUMP,
MDR-DUMP32, MDR-DUMPBLOCK, MDR-GETTAP, MDR-TAP.

Change the cartridge, in place, ignoring its write
protection: MDR-NEW, MDR-LABEL, MDR-ERASE, MDR-RENAME,
MDR-COPY, MDR-AUTORUN, MDR-NOAUTORUN, MDR-FIX, MDR-PUT,
MDR-PUTTEXT, MDR-PUT32, MDR-PUTBLOCK, MDR-PUTTAP.
