    UART-send-text>   a n --

Sends n bytes starting at a to the RPi0 over UART, one byte
at a time via UART-TX-BYTE, escaping any > (GT) byte in the
text by prefixing it with a backslash. Used by SEND-BLOCK to
transmit a BLOCK's content plus its trailing redirection
target without a stray > breaking the shell command.

See HELP SEND-BLOCK.

Available after NEEDS SEND-BLOCK
