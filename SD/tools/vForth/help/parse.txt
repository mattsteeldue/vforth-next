    PARSE  c -- a n

Parse the text up to the delimiter c from the input
stream and leave its address and length, inside the
input buffer. Unlike WORD, leading delimiters are not
skipped, so the text may be empty, and nothing is
copied to HERE. >IN moves past the delimiter; at the
end of the line the text stops there.
H", S", +" and PAD" parse with it: S" " is empty.
Forth-2012 CORE EXT. See also WORD.
