    AFX-CH-DESC  -- a

Address of the channel descriptor table used by AFXFRAME:
48 bytes, one 4-byte descriptor per voice, 9 voices
(3 AY chips x 3 channels), the last 12 bytes spare.
  +0  current address in the effect data; a zero high
      byte means the channel is free
  +2  effect timer
To start an effect on voice n (1-based):
  addr  AFX-CH-DESC n 1- 4 * +  !

Part of the AFXFRAME library.
Available after NEEDS AFXFRAME
