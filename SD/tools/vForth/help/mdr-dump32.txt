    MDR-DUMP32  -- cccc cccc cccc

Like MDR-DUMP, but the bytes are cut in lines of 32, each
ended by LF, its trailing blanks and NULs removed: the
32-column screens of the old Microdrive vForth become a
text file. The same as mdr.pl dump32=.

Read-only: the cartridge is never written.
Example: MDR-DUMP32 M2.MDR !Blocks BLOCKS.TXT

See MDR-DUMPBLOCK, MDR-DUMP. Available after NEEDS MDR
