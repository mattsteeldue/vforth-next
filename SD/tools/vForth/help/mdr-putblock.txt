    MDR-PUTBLOCK  -- cccc cccc cccc

Like MDR-PUT32, but driven by the "\ Screen# n" headers,
the reverse of MDR-DUMPBLOCK. Each header starts screen
n, of 16 lines: a shorter screen is completed with
blanks, lines past the 16th are dropped, and so are the
lines before the first header. Screens that were skipped
are written blank, 512 bytes each.

    MDR-PUTBLOCK M2.MDR !Blocks B.F

See MDR-DUMPBLOCK.
