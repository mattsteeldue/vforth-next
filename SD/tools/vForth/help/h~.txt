    H"  -- ha

Parse a string up to the next " and store it on Heap
as a counted string, returning its heap-address ha.
The zero cell HEAP lays after the data makes it a
counted z-string too: ha FAR 1+ is null-terminated,
as NextZXOS wants a file name. If the string does not
fit the current 8K page, HEAP moves to the next one
and ha is there. Use FAR just before reading it.
Part of heap facility. See also +" +C S" FAR.

Available after NEEDS H"
