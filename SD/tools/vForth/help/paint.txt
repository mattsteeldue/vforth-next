    PAINT  x y --

Flood-fills with the current colour ATTRIB the region
that contains the pixel (x,y), one row span at a time.
The boundary is decided by EDGE: in Layer 0 and Layer 1,1
-1,3 any ink pixel; in Layer 1,0 and Layer 2 any pixel of
the colour ATTRIB, so there the fill colour must be the
outline colour, or the fill floods the whole screen.
Pending spans live in an 8K page of their own (page 40,
reserved from NextZXOS while PAINT runs): up to 2048, and
a very jagged shape may be left partly unfilled.
[BREAK] stops the fill.

Not part of GRAPHICS: load it after the library with
NEEDS PAINT.  See also: PLOT  POINT.  Tutorial 038.
