    +"  ha -- ha

Parse a string up to the next " and append it to the
counted string at ha, extended in place. ha must be
the last block created on Heap, as just after H".
The string stays a counted z-string.
Errors: #-18 more than 255 characters, #14 ha is not
the last heap block, #12 the block would grow into
the tail of the page, offset $1F58 on (the UDG area).
Part of heap memory facility. See also H" +C.

Available after NEEDS +"
