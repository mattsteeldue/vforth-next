    MDR-FIX  -- cccc

Correct the wrong checksums of a .MDR cartridge image, as
mdr.pl -x, and tell how many sectors changed: header and
descriptor checksums of each sector, and the data
checksum of a bad sector only. A used sector whose data
does not match keeps its checksum, since the error there
is in the data: MDR-INFO shows it as da!.

    MDR-FIX M2.MDR

See MDR-INFO.
