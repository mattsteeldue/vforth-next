    F/MOD  fp1 fp2 -- fp3 fp4

Divides fp1 by fp2: fp4 is the whole quotient, rounded
down as FINT does, fp3 the remainder, fp1 - fp2*fp4.
Stops with "Division by zero." when fp2 is zero.
See also FMOD.

    FLOATING  7.0 2.0 F/MOD F. F.  \ 3.0000e0 1.0000e0

Part of the FLOATING library.
Available after NEEDS FLOATING
