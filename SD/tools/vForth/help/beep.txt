    BEEP  ms pitch --

Plays a note on the beeper, like the BASIC command BEEP.
ms is the duration in milliseconds; pitch is in
semitones from middle C: 0 = C, 9 = A 440 Hz, 12 = C one
octave above, negative values go below.
The CPU speed is set to 3.5 MHz while the note plays and
restored afterwards.

    500 9 BEEP

Part of the BLEEP library.
Available after NEEDS BLEEP
