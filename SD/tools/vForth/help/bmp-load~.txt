    BMP-LOAD"   --

IMMEDIATE. Loads a 256-color .bmp file straight into Layer 2
video RAM (six 8K pages via MMU7), checking the "BM"
signature and size bounds first. Filename is quote-delimited:

    BMP-LOAD" C:/demos/bmp256converts/bitmaps/future.bmp"

Works both interpreted and inside a colon-definition: at
compile time it compiles S" plus a call to (BMP-LOAD); at
the interpreter it opens and loads the file right away.
Switch to LAYER2 before loading and back to LAYER12 after,
e.g.  LAYER2  BMP-LOAD" ..."  WAIT-KEY  LAYER12 .

Available after NEEDS BMP-LOAD"
