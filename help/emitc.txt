    EMITC  c --

Sends the character c, all eight bits, to the current
channel through the ROM print routine (rst $10): no
translation and no masking, unlike EMIT, and OUT is not
updated. Codes 128-255 print block graphics, UDG and
tokens; codes 0-31 are the control codes of the ROM
(16 INK, 17 PAPER, 22 AT, ...) and take their arguments
from the next EMITCs. A bad argument (e.g. 99 for INK)
is a ROM error: the machine drops to BASIC, not to ok.
Use SELECT to change the current stream.

See also: EMIT  TYPE  SELECT
