    BMP-LOAD  a --

Loads a 256-colour 256x192 .bmp file straight into Layer 2
video RAM. a is the address of a counted z-string holding
the filename, the kind compiled by ," :

    CREATE PIC  ," tutorial/bmp/jaws.bmp"
    LAYER2  PIC BMP-LOAD  WAIT-KEY  LAYER12

The "BM" signature and the size are checked first.
Errors: #41 open, #46 read, #38 not a valid BMP, #45 seek.
See HELP BMP-LOAD" for the quote-delimited form.

Available after NEEDS BMP-LOAD
