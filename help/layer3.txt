    LAYER3  --

MARKER of the Layer 3 (tilemap) library: executing LAYER3
forgets the library. To use the tilemap do not call it,
call one of the mode words instead.
NEEDS LAYER3 loads:
  TILE-OFF  TILE-40  TILE-80  TILE-TXT
  TILE-40-PALETTE  TILE-80-PALETTE  TILE-TXT-PALETTE
  SET-PAL  GET-PAL  TILE-MODE:  TILE-PALETTE:
See tutorial 058.

Available after NEEDS LAYER3
