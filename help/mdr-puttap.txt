    MDR-PUTTAP  -- cccc cccc

Write all the files of a .TAP to a .MDR cartridge image,
in place. Cartridge and .TAP follow on the input line.
The sectors needed are counted first: error #65, and
nothing written, if they are not free. A name already on
the cartridge is skipped with a message; a wrong tape
checksum gives a warning (#34) and the file is written.

    MDR-PUTTAP M1.MDR ALL.TAP

See MDR-TAP.
