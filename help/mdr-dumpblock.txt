    MDR-DUMPBLOCK  -- cccc cccc cccc

Like MDR-DUMP32, but every 16 lines, i.e. every 512
bytes, a header is written: two empty lines, then
"\ Screen# n" counting from 1. The !Blocks file of an
old Microdrive vForth becomes a source file, one Screen
after the other. The same as mdr.pl dumpblock=.

Read-only: the cartridge is never written.
Example: MDR-DUMPBLOCK M2.MDR !Blocks BLOCKS.F

See MDR-DUMP32, MDR-DUMP. Available after NEEDS MDR
