    MDR-SECTORS  -- cccc

Like MDR-CAT, but each file is followed by the numbers of
its sectors, from the highest, eight at most (then ...).
Then the erased files: free sectors grouped by the name
they still hold, marked by "-" after it, and used files
of 0 bytes; the bad sectors; the totals, with "total
erased" too. The same as mdr.pl -l -s -f.

Read-only: the cartridge is never written.
Example: MDR-SECTORS M1.MDR

See MDR-CAT, MDR-INFO. Available after NEEDS MDR
