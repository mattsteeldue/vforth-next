    SPRITE-UPDATE  a n --

Sends the sprite structure at a (normally SPRITE) to the
hardware slot n, 0 to 127, and makes it visible: the four
attribute bytes go to port $57 after selecting the slot
on port $303B.
The slot is n, not the pattern: the pattern shown is the
field _spriteid. To animate, send different patterns to
the same slot.

Example:  SPRITE 0 SPRITE-UPDATE
Available after NEEDS SPRITE.  Tutorial 053.
