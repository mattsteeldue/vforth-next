    BEEP-PITCH  pitch -- hz8

Converts a pitch, in semitones from middle C as in BEEP,
into its frequency in Hz multiplied by 8, the value
BLEEP-CALC expects. The range is -60 (8.2 Hz) to 57
(7040 Hz); outside it, "Invalid numeric argument."

    9 BEEP-PITCH .               \ 3520 (440 Hz)

Part of the BLEEP library.
Available after NEEDS BLEEP
