    SPRITES-ON  --

Turns the sprite engine on: loads the identity palette
in the first sprite palette (colour i = i), sets $E3 as
the transparent colour (NextReg $14) and writes 3 to
NextReg $15, sprites visible and over the border.
NextReg $43 is left on the sprite palette: give 0 $43 REG!
before writing the ULA palette with $40/$41 again.
SPRITES-OFF turns the engine off.

Available after NEEDS SPRITE.  Tutorial 053.
