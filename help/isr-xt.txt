    ISR-XT  -- a

Address of the cell holding the xt of the definition run
at every 50Hz interrupt. Change it with interrupts off:

    ISR-OFF  ' cccc ISR-XT !  ISR-ON

Inside a colon-definition use ['] instead of ' .
The definition must leave the stack as it found it.

Part of the INTERRUPTS library.
Available after NEEDS INTERRUPTS
