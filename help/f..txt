    F.  fp --

Prints a floating-point number in scientific notation,
a mantissa, the letter e and an exponent, followed by a
space. PLACES sets the number of decimals (4 at load).

    FLOATING  1.5 F.             \ 1.5000e0
    2 PLACES  -1000.0 F.         \ -1.00e3

Part of the FLOATING library.
Available after NEEDS FLOATING
