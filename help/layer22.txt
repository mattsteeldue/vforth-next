    LAYER22  --

Activates Layer 2 high-res: 320x256 pixels, 256
colours, 1 colour per pixel (80K RAM, vertical
bands).  Re-vectors PLOT/POINT/DRAW-LINE/CIRCLE.
x = vertical 0..255, y = horizontal 0..319.
Text printed here comes out sideways: top to
bottom, lines from left to right, each
character transposed (turned and mirrored).
Draw here; type and read in LAYER12.

Part of the LAYER22-GRAPHICS library.
Available after NEEDS LAYER22-GRAPHICS
