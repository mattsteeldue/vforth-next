    *_   n1 n2 -- n3

Multiply two 12:4 fixed-point numbers (12 bits integer part,
4 bits fractional part, one 16-bit cell each). n3 is the
product, rescaled back to 12:4.

Geometrical/unsigned-multiply trick: both factors are offset
by $8000 before an unsigned 16x16 multiply, and the offset is
subtracted back out afterward, so no signed-multiply routine
is needed.

Available after NEEDS *_
