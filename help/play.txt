language: strings 1-3 on channels A, B, C of AY1,
4-6 on AY2, 7-9 on AY3. Waits until the music ends;
H in a string or BREAK stops it.

  notes c..b C..B, # sharp, $ flat, O0-O8 octave

  V0-V15 volume, U W0-W7 X envelope, M0-M63 mixer
  T60-T240 tempo (first string), ( ) repeat,
  ! comment !, H halt, L R S stereo
