    LAYER23-GRAPHICS  --

NEEDS LAYER23-GRAPHICS loads GRAPHICS-COMMON and
activates Layer 2 high-res: 640x256 pixels, 16
colours, 4 bits per pixel (two pixels per byte).
Uses 80K of RAM (ten 8K MMU7 pages) laid out in
vertical bands, the same bytes as LAYER22.
Same PLOT/DRAW-LINE/CIRCLE as the other modes;
x = vertical 0..255, y = horizontal 0..639.
The five 16K banks from NextReg $12 must be free.
