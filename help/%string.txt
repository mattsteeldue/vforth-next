    /STRING  a n k -- a+k n-k

Drop the first k characters of the string a n:
the address moves forward by k, the length
shrinks by as much. Nothing is copied.

    S" Spectrum" 2 /STRING TYPE   \ ectrum

A negative k gives back characters dropped
earlier. Keeping the first k characters is
just k MIN on the length.
