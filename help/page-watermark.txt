    PAGE-WATERMARK    -- n

Constant. The offset within an 8K MMU7 page (0..
$1FFF) beyond which SKIP-HP-PAGE forces HP on to the
start of the next page, leaving a small unusable tail
rather than splitting an entry across a page
boundary. The 257-byte tail ($1EFF-$1FFF) also
covers BASIC's UDG area, $FF58-$FFFF: a write meant
for the UDGs, made while MMU7 holds a heap page,
falls there and harms no entry. See also HP,
SKIP-HP-PAGE, FAR.
