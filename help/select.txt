    SELECT  n --

Makes stream n the current output: every word that
prints (EMIT, TYPE, . and ." among them) sends its
characters there. 2 is the screen, 3 the printer;
4 to 15 work only if BASIC opened them with OPEN #
before vForth started. Calls the ROM routine
CHAN-OPEN ($1601).

The screen comes back with VIDEO, and by itself when
vForth waits for a line at the prompt, after the ok:
end the line with VIDEO.  Example:
    3 SELECT 10 LIST VIDEO

See VIDEO, DEVICE.
