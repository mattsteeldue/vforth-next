    F>PAD  fp -- a n

Converts a floating-point number into the text that F.
prints, held in the pictured numeric output area as <#
... #> leaves it: address and length. The text is
overwritten by the next number printed.

    FLOATING  2.5 F>PAD TYPE     \ 2.5000e0

Part of the FLOATING library.
Available after NEEDS FLOATING
