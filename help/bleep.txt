    BLEEP  n1 n2 --

Calls the ROM beeper routine at $03B5 with raw
parameters, for a tone of f Hz lasting t seconds:
  n1 = f * t                    (number of cycles)
  n2 = ( 3500000 / f - 241 ) / 8        (pitch)
The ROM timing assumes a CPU speed of 3.5 MHz.
BLEEP-CALC computes the two numbers; BEEP does it all.

Available after NEEDS BLEEP
